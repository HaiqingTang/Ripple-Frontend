# DO/BE/FEEL Motivational Model  

## **Who (Stakeholders)**  

- Primary Users: Community volunteers and Enterprise
- Developers: Project team members
- Client: Industry partner

## **DO (Functional Goals)**  

- Interest Subscription
- Offline Meeting
- Challenge System 
- Reward System

## **BE (Quality Goals)**  

- Secure
- Accessible
- Scalable
- Usable
- Reliable
- Collaborative

## **FEEL (Emotional Goals - OPTIONAL)**  

- Confident
- Engaged
- Supported
- Motivated
