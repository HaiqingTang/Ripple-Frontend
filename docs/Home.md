## 📖 Project Overview
Our project, **RiPPLE**, is a design-led solution aimed at supporting early-career community service workers and preventing burnout, in the form of a mobile app.  
The app will provide:
- Quick wellbeing check-ins  
- Journaling for self-reflection  
- Peer connection via feed & discussion boards  
- Local meet-ups and challenges  
- Rewards and gamification for engagement  

👉 See the [Project Overview](Project-Overview) page for details on scope, goals, team roles, and timeline.
