# Welcome to the RP-Wombat wiki!

This Wiki contains important documentation for our project, including meeting notes, task tracking, and development progress.

The RP-Wombat project aims to support early-career social workers by providing a platform for emotional check-ins, quick journaling, and community engagement.
We collaborate closely with our industry partner to iteratively design and deliver an MVP that is both user-centered and technically feasible.
