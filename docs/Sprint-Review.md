Demonstration
- What was demonstrated:
  - Complete Figma prototype of the interest subscription & offline connections system
  - Fully designed challenge & reward system with gamification elements
  - User journey from discovery to participation in challenges and meetups
  - Reward redemption workflow with local business partnerships
- Demo Format: Presentation video showcasing the Figma prototype with voiceover explanation of key features and user flows.
- Feedback received:
  - Client praised the comprehensive approach to addressing burnout through peer connections
  - Positive feedback on the gamification of self-care activities
  - Appreciation for the focus on both digital and offline connections
  - Suggestion to emphasize the "Wellbeing that flows through you and beyond" concept more prominently
  - Request for additional consideration of data privacy in peer connection features

Stakeholder Feedback
- Positive feedback:
  - Excellent alignment with the mission of preventing burnout among community service workers
  - Strong focus on both emotional support (peer connections) and practical solutions (challenges & rewards)
  - Well-designed balance between organizational support and user autonomy
  - Effective translation of research insights into tangible features
  - Professional quality of Figma prototypes and documentation
- Suggestions:
  - Consider adding more emphasis on the mentorship aspect for early-career workers
  - Explore ways to make the onboarding process even more intuitive
  - Further develop the integration possibilities with existing wellness apps
  - Consider accessibility features for users experiencing high stress

Notes
- Overall, this sprint was highly successful in establishing the foundational design for RiPPLE. The team effectively translated the core mission of "Wellbeing that flows through you and beyond" into tangible features that address the three major factors of burnout: emotional exhaustion, lack of career support, and systematic pressures.
- The focus on both digital (challenge & reward system) and offline (meetups & connections) solutions demonstrates a holistic approach to wellbeing. By normalizing self-care through gamification and strengthening peer connections through shared activities, RiPPLE is well-positioned to help early-career community service workers like Ellie thrive in their important work.
