---

### Discussion Points & Notes  

#### Prototype Features  
- Login page and personal page reviewed.  
- “How’s your mood?” → need a way to visualise mood across **week to month**.  
- Mood chart is a **good-to-have** visual, not just text description.  

#### Journaling  
- Current questions too ambiguous:  
  - “How would you rate your day overall?”  
  - “How are you feeling today?”  
  - “Do you want to journal?”  
- Client suggested adding more prompts, e.g.:  
  - *“What’s on your mind?”*  
  - *“Have you tried something new?”*   
- New feature (Could have): if user reports **low mood for 3 days consistently**, app prompts with supportive questions.  
- Journaling page can be **separate**, with **categories/topic tagging**.  
- After journaling → option to tag topic → save journal.  

#### Mood Function  
- Current **smiley face icon** may need redesign.  
- Add option to **skip mood logging page** when opening the app.  
- Frequency of mood questions configurable in **settings** (default = daily).  

#### Discussion Page  
- Should be focused on **topics of interest** (sharing platforms).  
- Helps users find others with **similar interests**.  
- Proposed as the **landing/home page** instead of mood log.  

#### User Profile  
- Mood chart should be accessible from profile.  
- Journaling tags can appear as a **top tags list**.  

#### Subscription Model  
- Organisations can subscribe to provide access for employees.  
- Important for younger workers who may be uncomfortable reaching out directly to managers.  

#### Priorities & Application Flow  
- **iOS development** prioritised.  
- Landing page = **Discussion page** (home).  
- On first install → introduction/tutorial (what the app is, how to use it).  
- Home page should act as **community hub** with clickable key features.  
- Send **Figma prototype link** to client for confirmation, and ask for feedback timeline.  

---

### Decisions  
- Journaling will include improved prompts, with randomisation and tagging system.  
- Mood log will be **optional** (skip available).  
- Landing/home page will be the **Discussion page**.  
- Mood chart accessible from **Profile page**.  
- Frequency of mood questions customisable in settings.  
- Prioritise iOS development for MVP.  
- Client will review Figma prototype once shared, with deadline to be confirmed.
