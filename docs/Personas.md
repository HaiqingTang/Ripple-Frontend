# Persona 1: Ellie 
## 1.1. Basic Information

* Full Name:  Ellie Thompson
* Role/Occupation: Youth Caseworker
* Age Group: 20-25
* Location: Sydney,Australia
* Tech Proficiency: Medium
* Background: Ellie is a 23-year-old recent graduate who entered the community services sector with high ideals and a strong desire to make a difference. She works as a youth caseworker, supporting at-risk young people. While passionate about her work, she's experiencing the early signs of burnout and feeling increasingly isolated despite her natural tendency to thrive on connection

* Build meaningful connections with peers who understand the challenges of community work
* Feel valued and supported in her career path

* Waking up feeling groggy, disconnected, and emotionally exhausted
* Feeling isolated from colleagues and lacking peer support
* Struggling to maintain work-life balance with overwhelming caseloads
* Life feels full of obligations with little time for personal care
* Experiencing early signs of burnout but unsure how to address it
Behaviour and Needs

## 1.3. Preferred Communication Channels: Peer-to-peer platforms
* Information Sources: Social media feeds, peer recommendations, wellness blogs, professional networks
* Common Tools & Technologies Used: Smartphone apps, social platforms, Case management software
* Interaction with the System: Seeks quick, accessible tools that can be used on-the-go

## 1.4. User Stories (For This Persona)

1. As a community service worker, I want to connect with peers who understand my challenges, so that I can feel less isolated in my work.

Additional Notes:<br>
Ellie represents the primary target user - young, idealistic community service workers who are at risk of early burnout. She values connection and community but struggles with isolation and exhaustion.
