# Persona: Jackson Hu

Basic Information

- **Full Name:** Jackson Hu
- **Role/Occupation:** Chief Executive Officer (CEO), Melbourne’s largest technology company
- **Age Group:** 30-45
- **Location:** Melbourne, Australia
- **Tech Proficiency:** High

**Background**  
Jackson has over 20 years of experience in the tech industry and currently leads one of Melbourne's most influential tech companies. His responsibilities include developing long-term strategy, managing thousands of employees, and ensuring innovation aligns with business growth. Recently, his company has expanded its partnerships with the government and non-profit sectors, where employee well-being and retention are key challenges.

Jackson is particularly interested in solutions that can support the mental health and resilience of young workers, particularly in high-pressure industries. He sees RiPPLE not only as a wellness tool but also as a method for cultivating a sustainable, productive, and engaged workforce.



Goals and Motivations
- Increase employee engagement and well-being.
- Understand employee health and well-being.
- Raise company awareness through partnerships with RiPPLE.



Pain Points and Challenges
- Employees are distracted from their primary work to use RIPPLE.
- Shifting employees' original plans due to RIPPLE can have negative consequences.
- Extensive data analysis is needed to prove the viability of RIPPLE.



Behaviour and Needs
- **Preferred Communication Channels:** Executive reports, leadership briefings, board-level presentations. 
- **Information Sources:** Industry research reports, HR analytics dashboards, professional networks.  
- **Common Tools & Technologies Used:** Enterprise analytics platforms, HR systems, corporate communication tools (Slack, MS Teams).  
- **Interaction with the System:** 
Uses RiPPLE’s organization-level dashboard to track wellbeing trends across teams. Drive adoption of RiPPLE across departments as part of wellbeing strategy.



> "As a CEO, I want to care about the physical and mental well-being of nearly all my employees, so that I can be confident they can do their jobs well."

> "As a business leader, I want to reduce attrition among early employees, so that my company can retain talent and maintain productivity."


Additional Notes

Jackson represents the leadership perspective:

He isn't a daily user of the RiPPLE logbook or check-in system, but rather a decision-maker who ensures its implementation.

His top priorities are sustainable impact and employee retention.

# Persona: York

Basic Information

- **Full Name:** York
- **Role/Occupation:** Early-career social service worker 
- **Age Group:** 18-25
- **Location:** Melbourne
- **Tech Proficiency:** Medium

**Background**  
York is a recent graduate entering the social services industry. Passionate and eager to help others, he quickly realized the demands of the environment were far greater than those of university. He faced the harsh realities of social work, balancing client interactions, demanding documentation requirements, and the emotional strain of working with vulnerable populations.

He constantly compared himself to his more experienced colleagues and felt pressure to bridge the gap. York sometimes questioned his suitability for the profession and worried about burnout during his first year.



Goals and Motivations
- Adapt smoothly to a professional environment. 
- Be able to confidently interact with clients.
- Maintain a healthy balance between work and personal well-being.



Pain Points and Challenges
- I felt overwhelmed by the differences between school and real work.  
- The project was complex and large, and I worried about making mistakes.



Behaviour and Needs
- **Preferred Communication Channels:** Email, Slack/Teams.
- **Information Sources:** Chats with colleagues, WhatsApp.
- **Common Tools & Technologies Used:** MS Office, Journaling apps.
- **Interaction with the System:** Record daily check-ins to reflect on stress and confidence levels. Participate in challenges to establish positive daily habits.



User Stories (For This Persona)
> “As a new social worker, I want to track my stress levels so I know if I’m making progress or burning out.”

> "As a new employee, I want to be mentally and physically healthy and use coping strategies to handle the overwhelming workload."



Additional Notes
York represents the youngest and most vulnerable segment of the social sector workforce—recent graduates facing the realities of the workforce.

# Persona: William

Basic Information

- **Full Name:** William
- **Role/Occupation:** Founder of a non-profit foundation supporting social workers
- **Age Group:** 45-60
- **Location:** Melbourne
- **Tech Proficiency:** High

**Background**  
William founded a foundation dedicated to improving the well-being of social workers. Over the years, he has witnessed many young professionals leave the profession due to burnout, stress, and lack of support. His foundation is dedicated to raising funds and developing programs to support the well-being of social workers.

William is committed to ensuring that social workers feel supported not only financially but also emotionally. He is exploring how to integrate RiPPLE into his foundation's initiatives, creating a scalable digital platform for monitoring and improving the well-being of social workers.



Goals and Motivations
- Provide benefits for social service workers.
- Ensure mental health and well-being are considered core elements of corporate support.



Pain Points and Challenges
- Difficulty engaging young professionals in its benefits program.  
- Lack of real-time insights.



Behaviour and Needs
- **Preferred Communication Channels:** Professional reports, Emails, Meetings.
- **Information Sources:** Sector research reports, Wellbeing surveys, Government data.
- **Common Tools & Technologies Used:** Salesforce Nonprofit Cloud, Tableau
- **Interaction with the System:** Use RiPPLE's aggregated data dashboard to track well-being trends. View RiPPLE as a way to demonstrate accountability to donors and stakeholders.



User Stories (For This Persona)
> "As a foundation founder, I want to understand well-being trends across organizations so I can target resources where they're most needed."

> "As an industry advocate, I want to promote digital tools like RiPPLE so employees can feel supported in both their financial and emotional well-being."



Additional Notes
Unlike frontline social workers, William doesn't log in or check the RiPPLE platform every day. He is both an advocate and stakeholder.
