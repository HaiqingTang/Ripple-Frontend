## Client Feedback
- **Interest Subscription**: Users must clearly see whether topics are preset or user-generated.  
- **Meet-ups**: No anonymity allowed; real-name mechanism required for publishing and joining.  
- **Challenge System**: Concerns about self-reported data; mentor suggested potential technical validation (e.g., Apple Watch) but acknowledged this is future scope.  
- **Rewards System**: Liked the idea; stressed offers must come from genuine business partnerships for credibility and appeal.  

---

## Team Reflection
The team actively listened to client concerns and agreed to:
- Emphasize clarity regarding user-generated versus pre-set topics in the user interface.
- Consider implementing real-name requirements for meetups in future iterations.
- Recognize the limitations of self-reported challenge data and be mindful of possible future integrations with devices.
- Ensure rewards involve real business partnerships to maintain the system's credibility.
