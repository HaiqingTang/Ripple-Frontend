# Project Name: RiPPLE

---

## 1. Executive Summary
RiPPLE is a comprehensive mobile application designed to address burnout among millennial community service workers in Australia. It provides features for peer-to-peer connection, flexible self-care strategies, and community-driven support, creating an ecosystem where workers feel supported and connected. By normalising self-care and strengthening peer networks through simple, accessible tools, RiPPLE aims to reduce burnout risk and foster a culture where employee wellbeing is valued as much as client care. Ultimately, it’s about creating a supportive community of workers who feel empowered, and shifting workplace culture to value employee wellbeing just as much as client care.

---

## 2. Project Scope and Background

### 2.1. Problem Statement
*  **What?** - Burnout is particularly prevalent in the community services sector, where staff are routinely exposed to emotionally taxing situations and high pressure situations. 
* **Why?** - Community services organisations, while focused on prioritising client outcomes over staff wellbeing, often lack the resources or capacity to support the needs and wellbeing of their staff.
* **Impact** - As a result, young frontline workers often experience emotional exhaustion, disconnection from work and diminished feelings of accomplishment, leading to low retention rate of employees in the community services sector, lower productivity and declining morale.

### 2.2. Client Goals and Motivations
**Client Introduction**<br>
The RiPPLE project is supported by Mirela, a strategy and innovation consultant with extensive experience in the for-profit and health care sectors. Acting as an industry partner for the University of Melbourne final-year IT students, the client brings expertise in creating supportive environments where innovative teams can thrive.<br>
Through collaboration with students and research into the social services workforce, the client identified a critical gap: burnout disproportionately affects early-career frontline workers in the social sector, who often feel isolated, unsupported, and disillusioned.<br>

**Motivation:**  
Mirela is passionate about addressing this issue by:  

- Supporting the next generation of social service workers in building resilience  
- Providing tools for holistic wellbeing that empower individuals to thrive rather than exit the sector  
- Creating a sustainable community platform that helps reduce professional isolation  

**Goal:** 
1. Develop a functional Mobile MVP for RiPPLE Platform
2. Enable user experience testing to validate whether peer connection and wellbeing-focused tools can reduce burnout risk<br>

**Objective:** Create a working minimum viable product for an iOS mobile app that includes all core features for user experience testing<br>

**Specific Deliverables:** Mobile application with 7 core features:<br>
       (1) wellbeing check-in that can track emotional and energy levels<br>
       (2) journaling feature where users can drop in their thoughts on the go<br>
       (3) community feed for members to post useful and uplifting tips<br>
       (4) local events & connecting with others in person<br>
       (5) selecting topics of interest to get feeds (i.e. fitness, art, nutrition etc)<br>
       (6) joining different challenges<br>
       (7) rewards (i.e. discounts by local businesses (i.e. gym membership, yoga classes etc)<br>

**Justification:** This MVP directly addresses the gap identified in the client's previous research - no existing solution combines sector-specific understanding, peer-driven support, and accessible technology for community service workers. Success with this targeted MVP would help serve as a Proof-of-Concept for testing the fundamental hypothesis - that peer connection and through this platform can meaningfully reduce professional isolation and burnout risk.

### 2.3. Project Scope (Team Deliverables)
Our goal is to develop a minimum viable product (MVP), specifically focusing on four of the core features: user authentication system, profile management system, journaling, and community discussion board.<br>

_Note: RP Team Wombat will be focusing on delivering the other core features for this application_
* What are we building?<br>
   Our team will be focusing on developing an MVP mobile app including four essential features:
   * **User Authentication System** - Account creation, login, and user profile management
   * **Profile Management System:** - View profile stats and edit account details
   * **Journaling:** Quick daily check-ins to track emotional patterns and journaling to log thoughts and reflect on experiences.
   * **Discussion Boards:** Spaces for open dialogue to reduce isolation and share experiences.

* How are we building it?
   * **Version Control & Documentation:** GitHub (including Wiki for project documentation)  
   * **Design & Prototyping:** Figma (for wireframes and UI/UX design)  
   * **Frontend:** A cross-platform framework suitable for iOS (and adaptable to Android in future)  
   * **Backend:** A server-side framework with database support for user data and content storage  
   * **Infrastructure:** Free-tier cloud services and open-source tools wherever possible  

### 2.4. Impact
RiPPLE is not only about individual change, but also about transforming workplace culture. By encouraging early intervention and peer-driven wellbeing practices, the platform aims to:
1.  Reduce isolation and disengagement among workers.
2.  Encourage long-term career retention in the community service sector.
3.  Promote a culture of care, balance, and empowerment.

### 2.5. Success Metrics
1. All core features functional and ready for user experience testing
2. Application performs reliably on an iOS mobile application
3. A solution architecture that is maintainable, easily extendable with new features, and adaptable to other platforms such as Android

## 3. Project Constraints and Assumptions
### 3.1. Development Team Limitations
* Team Size: 5-person student development team with varying technical experience levels
* Experience Level: Academic project team with limited industry experience in full-stack development
* Available Time: Part-time development capacity due to concurrent academic commitments and coursework

### 3.2. Technology and Infrastructure Constraints
* Budget Limitations: Zero budget allocation requiring exclusive use of free-tier services and open-source solutions
* Third-Party Integration: Limited to free APIs and services, restricting advanced features like payment processing or enterprise integrations
* Performance Expectations: Must work within free-tier service limitations (potential slower load times, limited concurrent users)

### 3.3. Timeline Constraints
* Fixed Duration: Exactly 12 weeks from project initiation to final delivery, non-negotiable due to semester structure
* Holiday Periods: Development timeline includes university break periods potentially affecting team availability
