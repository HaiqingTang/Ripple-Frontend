## Project Goal
_To create a design-led solution that empowers early-career community service workers to prevent burnout through normalized self-care practices, meaningful peer connections, and accessible wellbeing strategies—ultimately helping organizations retain their millennial workforce and foster a thriving culture of wellbeing.

## Project Background & Scope

### Background
Burnout is more prevalent among social service workers than in other sectors, and early-career frontline workers are at the highest risk of leaving. Many young workers report feeling isolated, unsupported, and disillusioned despite engaging in meaningful community work. RiPPLE aims to address this by promoting peer support, normalizing self-care, and fostering community connections.

### Goals
- **Wellbeing Monitoring:** Provide workers with quick daily check-ins (energy, emotion) and journaling to track stress.  
- **Peer Learning & Sharing:** Enable workers to exchange tips, articles, podcasts, and wellbeing practices.  
- **Interest & Challenge Engagement:** Allow workers to subscribe to wellbeing topics, join challenges, and build healthy habits.  
- **Social Connection:** Facilitate offline meetups (coffee, walks, catch-ups) to reduce isolation.  
- **Recognition & Rewards:** Motivate self-care by partnering with local businesses to provide discounts and rewards.  
- **Organisational Insight:** Support leadership and non-profit partners with aggregated data on wellbeing trends.

### Problem Scope
RiPPLE's MVP focuses on four core features (RP-Wombat Team):
1. **Interest Subscription** (topics, updates, recommendations)
2. **Offline Connection** (meetups, RSVPs, location-based social networking)
3. **Challenge System** (habit formation, health goals, fundraisers)
4. **Reward System** (redeemable rewards tied to challenges)

### Constraints
- **Technical:** Limited to MVP scope within a 12-week semester, prioritising usability and stability over advanced features.  
- **Resource:** Client provides domain guidance but not technical implementation; development must be student-led.  
- **Time:** Only two development sprints available after planning (Sprints 2–3).  
- **Validation:** Prototypes and MVP features must be tested with client feedback, not external deployment.
