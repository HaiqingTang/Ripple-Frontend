# DO/BE/FEEL Motivational Model  

## **Who (Stakeholders)**  
List the primary stakeholders interacting with your system (e.g., users, administrators, developers). 
* **Early-career community service workers / organisations (primary users)** – use the app for self-care, wellbeing tracking, and peer support
* **Support staff** - use the app, and provide wellbeing support if needed
* **Administrators** – manage user accounts, ensure the app's safety and compliance
* **Developers** – implement, maintain, enhance, and scale the system
* **Client & Teaching staff** – review progress, validate goals, and ensure alignment with requirements

## **DO (Functional Goals)**  
Identify key features and functionality that the system should accomplish.

* 🎯 **User Authentication** – secure registration, login, and session management
* 🎯 **Profile Management** – view and edit personal profile details and preferences
* 🎯 **Daily Mood & Sleep Logging** – record daily mood, sleep quality, and duration, view historical patterns
* 🎯 **Personal Journaling** – record text or voice journal entries for self-reflection
* 🎯 **Community Discussion System** – browse, search, create posts, and engage with peers (likes)
* 🎯 **Wellbeing Engagement (future extension)** – daily check-ins, challenges, and gamification for motivation

## **BE (Quality Goals)**  
Identify key quality attributes that describe how the system should behave.
 
* **Secure** → Protect user data with basic data protection and privacy compliance (GDPR-aligned practices where feasible)
* **Accessible** → Mobile-first, user-friendly design with basic accessibility support
* **Scalable** → Modular architecture designed for future growth
* **Reliable** → App response time under 2 seconds; stable availability
* **Usable** → Simple and intuitive interface requiring no training
* **Maintainable** → Modular codebase for easy updates and feature extensions

## **FEEL (Emotional Goals - OPTIONAL)**  
How should stakeholders feel when interacting with the system? 

- **Confident** →  Trust the app functions are as expected
- **Engaged** →  Motivated to log moods, journal, and participate in the community
- **Supported** →  Feel connected to peers and not isolated in their work
- **Motivated** →  Encouraged by gamification, rewards, and challenges
- **Encouraged** →  Feel better wellbeing and professional resilience
- **Satisfied** →  Believe the app is practical, helpful, and improves work-life balance
